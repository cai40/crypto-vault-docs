Privacy Policy

Crypto Vault · Last updated: June 6, 2026

Crypto Vault (“the app”) is a local-only encrypted media vault for iPhone. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

Summary

  • No account, no cloud sync, no backend servers operated by us.
  • Your files and PIN-derived keys stay on your device.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • Subscriptions are processed by Apple, not by us directly.

Information stored on your device

When you use the app, data is stored locally on your iPhone, including:

  • Encrypted files you import (photos, videos, documents, and other supported types).
  • Encrypted thumbnails generated for gallery preview.
  • Folder names and file metadata (such as file names and types) needed to organize your vault.
  • A random encryption salt and trial start date in local app storage.

Your PIN is never stored. It is used only to derive an encryption key in memory while the vault is unlocked. If you forget your PIN, we cannot recover your files.

What we do not collect

We do not operate servers that receive your vault contents. We do not collect:

  • Your imported photos, videos, or documents
  • Your PIN or encryption keys
  • Location data
  • Contacts or browsing history
  • Advertising identifiers for tracking

Photo library access

The app requests access to your photo library only when you tap + to import files. Selected items are encrypted on-device before being saved in the vault. We do not upload them to our servers.

Subscriptions and purchases

Crypto Vault offers an optional subscription and a one-time lifetime purchase through Apple In-App Purchase. Payment, billing, and subscription management are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy. We do not receive your payment card details.

A 3-month free trial is tracked on your device. After the trial, you may subscribe or purchase lifetime access to continue using the app.

Encryption

Files are encrypted on your device using industry-standard algorithms (PBKDF2 key derivation and AES-GCM) before being stored. Decrypted content exists in memory only while you view or export files.

Export and deletion

You can export decrypted files from the app when you choose. Deleting files or folders removes them from local storage. Uninstalling the app or clearing app data may permanently remove access to encrypted content if the local salt or vault data is lost.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

Contact

Questions about privacy or the app: GitHub Issues or see our Support page.
