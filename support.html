Crypto Vault Support

Local-only encrypted media vault for iPhone

Get help

The fastest way to reach us is open a GitHub Issue. Bug reports, feature requests, and subscription questions are welcome.

Before you write

  • App version (e.g. 0.1.0) and build number from TestFlight or the App Store
  • iOS version (e.g. iOS 18)
  • What you expected vs what happened
  • Screenshots if something looks wrong in the UI

Please do not include your PIN, passwords, or private photos in public issues.

Common topics

Forgot PIN

Crypto Vault cannot recover a forgotten PIN. There is no cloud backup of your key. If you still know your PIN, unlock normally. If not, the only option is to reset the vault, which permanently erases encrypted content on that device.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled by Apple. Use Plans in the app or iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions to manage or cancel. Tap Restore purchases on the paywall after reinstalling.

Import or storage issues

Very large imports may take time while files encrypt. If import fails, try fewer or smaller files. Low device storage can also block saves.
